about pixel_

Pixel Computer Club offers a modern gaming experience at 36 Mangilik El Avenue in Astana.

More than just a traditional computer club, Pixel is a comfortable and welcoming space for gamers of all levels. With powerful gaming PCs, modern equipment and a friendly atmosphere, Pixel is the perfect place to play, practice and spend time with friends.

Whether you come to enjoy your favorite games, improve your skills, meet other players or compete in tournaments, Pixel Computer Club brings Astana’s gaming community together in one place.

Our club is designed for both casual players and dedicated esports fans. From everyday gaming sessions and team practices to tournaments, boot camps, birthday parties and community events, Pixel provides the right environment for every type of gamer.

Pixel Computer Club is where technology, competition and entertainment come together. Visit us at 36 Mangilik El Avenue, Astana, and experience gaming at Pixel.
